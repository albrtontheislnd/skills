# Vietnam-Specific Economic Tracking

Pan-Asia wires (Nikkei, Reuters) cover Vietnam intermittently — for consistent Vietnam-focused tracking, use these primary and local sources.

## Primary data

| Source | Best for | URL |
|---|---|---|
| Vietnam General Statistics Office (GSO) | Primary data release calendar — CPI, GDP, trade balance, FDI | https://www.gso.gov.vn/en/homepage/ |
| State Bank of Vietnam (SBV) | Monetary policy, exchange rate management | https://www.sbv.gov.vn/webcenter/portal/en |
| Ministry of Industry and Trade (MOIT) | Trade policy, export/import data | https://en.moit.gov.vn |

## English-language local business press

| Source | Best for | URL |
|---|---|---|
| VnExpress International — Business | General business/economy news, fast local turnaround | https://e.vnexpress.net/news/business |
| Vietnam Investment Review (VIR) | FDI flows, investment climate, policy analysis | https://vir.com.vn |
| Vietnam Briefing (Dezan Shira) | Regulatory/tax/trade explainer content for foreign investors | https://www.vietnam-briefing.com |

## What to watch specifically

- **FDI registration/disbursement figures** (monthly, from GSO/MOIT) — leading indicator for manufacturing/export capacity
- **Export/import balance with US, China, EU** — Vietnam's trade profile is unusually exposed to US tariff policy and China-plus-one supply chain shifts
- **SBV reference exchange rate (VND/USD) band adjustments** — signals monetary stance
- **PMI (S&P Global Vietnam Manufacturing PMI)** — monthly, widely cited leading indicator, released via S&P Global / Reuters

## Cross-reference

Cambodia-Vietnam GDP convergence and broader Mekong trade dynamics: check ADB Data Portal (see `sources.md`) alongside GSO — ADB's cross-country series make Vietnam-Cambodia-Laos comparisons easier than GSO's Vietnam-only data.
